# Product Guidelines

## Prose Style
The preferred prose style for all documentation and user-facing content is **Clear and Concise**. This approach emphasizes brevity and directness, avoiding jargon wherever possible. The goal is to ensure that information is easily understood and actionable, particularly for technical documentation where clarity is paramount.

## Messaging and Tone
-   **Direct:** Get straight to the point without unnecessary preamble.
-   **Accurate:** Ensure all information is factually correct and up-to-date.
-   **Unambiguous:** Use precise language to prevent misinterpretation.
-   **Action-Oriented:** Focus on what the user needs to do or understand.

## Formatting
-   Use headings and subheadings to break up content and improve readability.
-   Utilize bullet points and numbered lists for easy digestion of information.
-   Employ bold text for emphasis on key terms or actions.
